About Us

We are a team of undergraduate engineering students who set out to make medical information easier to understand and more accessible for everyone. Many people receive medical reports, prescriptions, and scan results that are difficult to interpret without medical knowledge. Our goal is to bridge this gap using technology.

This web application is designed to help users understand their medical reports, learn about the medicines they take, track their health history, and ask health-related questions in a simple and user-friendly manner. By combining structured medical data with intelligent analysis, we aim to present complex information in clear, understandable language.

We believe that technology should empower individuals to be more informed about their own health. While this platform does not replace professional medical advice, it serves as a supportive tool to help users better understand and organize their health information.

This project is developed as part of an academic initiative and reflects our learning, research, and interest in building meaningful, real-world applications using software engineering and artificial intelligence.

What We Do

Our platform offers five core features built to make health information more accessible:

Medical Report Analyzer — Upload your lab reports and get clear, easy-to-understand explanations. We highlight abnormal values, explain what they mean, and present insights in simple language — even in your regional language.

Medicine Lookup — Search any medicine by brand name (like Dolo or Crocin) or generic name to understand its uses, how it works, and possible side effects. Powered by OpenFDA and AI fallback so you always get an answer.

Scan Report Analyzer — Upload X-ray or scan images and receive simplified explanations and visual insights to help you understand them better.

AI Health Query Assistant — Ask health-related questions and get reliable, easy-to-understand answers based on medical knowledge — without diagnosis or prescriptions.

Trend & History Tracking — Track your health over time. View trends and changes in your medical reports, compare past results, and gain insights into improvements or areas that need attention.

Who We Built This For

We built Medinsight AI for everyone — but especially for families where not every member has easy access to medical guidance.

Many senior citizens receive lab reports or prescriptions that they cannot interpret on their own, and may not have a smartphone or internet access to look things up. A family member with a single account can create up to five patient sub-accounts, making it easy to manage and track health records for parents, grandparents, or other family members — all from one place.

We also built this for students, caregivers, and anyone who has ever stared at a medical report and felt overwhelmed. You deserve to understand your own health.
