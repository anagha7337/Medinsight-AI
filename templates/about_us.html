About Us

We are a team of undergraduate engineering students who set out to make medical information easier to understand and more accessible for everyone. Many people receive medical reports, prescriptions, and scan results that are difficult to interpret without medical knowledge. Our goal is to bridge this gap using technology.

This web application is designed to help users understand their medical reports, learn about the medicines they take, track their health history, and ask health-related questions in a simple and user-friendly manner. By combining structured medical data with intelligent analysis, we aim to present complex information in clear, understandable language.

We believe that technology should empower individuals to be more informed about their own health. While this platform does not replace professional medical advice, it serves as a supportive tool to help users better understand and organize their health information.

This project is developed as part of an academic initiative and reflects our learning, research, and interest in building meaningful, real-world applications using software engineering and artificial intelligence.
