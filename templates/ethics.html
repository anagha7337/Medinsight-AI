Ethical Considerations

MedInsight AI is developed with a strong commitment to ethical principles, user safety, and responsible use of technology. The platform is designed to assist users in understanding medical information; however, it does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or professional healthcare advice. Users are strongly encouraged to consult qualified medical practitioners for any health-related concerns or decisions.

The system ensures transparency in its functioning by clearly communicating the limitations of AI-generated insights. All outputs are intended solely for informational and educational purposes and are presented in a simplified and non-alarming manner to avoid unnecessary anxiety or misinterpretation.

User privacy and data security are treated with utmost importance. Personal and medical data shared within the platform are handled securely. Data collection and usage are strictly limited to enhancing user experience and improving system performance.

The platform adheres to principles of fairness and inclusivity by providing multilingual support, thereby making healthcare information accessible to a wider and diverse audience. Efforts are made to minimize bias in AI-generated responses and ensure that the information provided is general, neutral, and non-discriminatory.

MedInsight AI follows a hybrid approach combining rule-based systems and AI models to ensure reliability and reduce the risk of misinformation. Continuous monitoring and improvements are undertaken to enhance accuracy, accountability, and ethical compliance.

By using this platform, users acknowledge and agree to its intended purpose as an assistive tool and not a substitute for professional medical consultation.
